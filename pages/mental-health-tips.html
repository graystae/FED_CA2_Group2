Mindfulness Practices

Try one of these next time you feel overwhelmed.

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Repeat 4 times to calm your nervous system.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 you taste — brings you back to the present.

Gratitude Journaling

Write down 3 things you're grateful for each night — shifts focus away from stressors.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Tense and release each muscle group from toes to head to release physical tension.
