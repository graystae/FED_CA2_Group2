Sleep Better, Feel Better

Good sleep is the foundation everything else is built on. Start with one small change tonight. 😴

Why Sleep Matters

Between school, work, screens and social life, sleep is often the first thing sacrificed in Singapore's fast-paced routine. But poor sleep worsens mood, focus, immunity and even appetite regulation. Most teens and young adults need 7–9 hours of sleep a night — yet many regularly get far less.

Body Repair

Sleep allows our body to rest and repair

Strong Immunity

Sleep allows our body to produce hormones to help fight off infections

Memory Consolidation

Sleeping allows the brain to remember better.

Emotional Balance

Sleeping allows our brain to reset to our emotional baseline

Recommended Sleep by Age

Age Group Recommended Sleep Common Challenge
Teens (13–18) 8–10 hours Early school start times
Young Adults (18–25) 7–9 hours Late-night screen use, social life
Adults (26–30) 7–9 hours Work stress, irregular schedules
