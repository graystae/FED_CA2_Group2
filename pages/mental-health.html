Take Care of Your Mind

In Singapore's competitive pace, managing stress isn't a luxury — it's essential.

Recognising Stress

Stress can show up as trouble concentrating, irritability, tension headaches, or sleep problems. Left unmanaged, chronic stress affects both mental and physical health. The good news: small daily practices can meaningfully reduce it.

Breathing Exercises

Slow, deep breathing activates your body's relaxation response in minutes.

Journaling

Writing down worries helps externalise them and reduce mental load.

Talking It Out

Sharing with a trusted friend, family member, or counsellor can lighten the load.
