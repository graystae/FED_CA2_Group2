Your Wind-Up Routine

Five simple steps to help your body and mind prepare for real rest.

  • 1

    Set a screen curfew (60 min before bed)

    Blue light and stimulating content delay your body's release of melatonin, the sleep hormone.

  • 2

    Dim the lights

    Switch to warm, low lighting to signal to your brain that night has arrived.

  • 3

    Do something calming

    Light stretching, journaling or reading a physical book all help you shift out of "alert" mode.

  • 4

    Cool your room down

    A slightly cool room (around 24–26°C in Singapore's climate) supports deeper sleep.

  • 5

    Keep a consistent bedtime

    Going to bed at roughly the same time each night trains your body to fall asleep faster over time.
